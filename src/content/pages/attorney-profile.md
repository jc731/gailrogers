      <h2>Empathetic and professional family law services</h2>
      <p>When the law touches family relationships, the legal process becomes deeply personal. To help you navigate emotionally challenging disputes with your spouse or other close relatives, I personally manage every aspect of your case. I regularly handle the following family law issues:</p>
      <h3>Divorce</h3>
      <p>If you have decided to dissolve your marriage, I can explain the divorce process. Illinois is a no-fault state with provisions for a simplified divorce. I often help clients negotiate marital settlements to obtain uncontested divorces, but am fully prepared to litigate when necessary.</p>
      <h3>Child Custody</h3>
      <p>I develop tailored parenting plans to fit your circumstances. I can also litigate child custody issues when necessary to protect your rights to share in raising your children.</p>
      <h3>Child Support</h3>
      <p>Illinois has state guidelines for basic child support, but many couples need to customize a support agreement for matters such as health insurance, private school tuition, and enrichment activities. I analyze your finances and make the strongest case for a fair outcome for you and your children.</p>
      <h3>Spousal Support</h3>
      <p>Whether you are the payer or the recipient, spousal support is a significant factor in your financial well-being. I gather all relevant facts to make a compelling case for fair and adequate support.</p>
      <p>On these and many other family law matters, I am prepared to work tirelessly for the results you deserve.</p>
